04 / THE HUMAN FACTOR

Small habits can create
big risks.

Water safety does not stop at treatment. Containers, hands, taps and filling areas can all become part of the journey.

01
🧴

Dirty bottles

Reusable bottles need regular cleaning. Residues and moisture can support microbial growth.

→ CLEAN REGULARLY
02
🖐️

Dirty hands

Touching bottle openings, taps or dispensing points with unclean hands can introduce contamination.

→ WASH HANDS
03
🚰

Unclean filling areas

Dirty surfaces, standing water and damaged fixtures can turn a clean source into a hygiene concern.

→ KEEP AREAS CLEAN
04
🔄

Sharing bottles

Sharing personal drinking containers can increase the opportunity for transfer of microbes between people.

→ USE YOUR OWN
